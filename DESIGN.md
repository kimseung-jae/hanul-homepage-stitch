## Brand & Style

This design system serves a dual constituency: middle-aged neighborhood business owners who demand friction-free readability, and municipal or public stakeholders assessing civic stability and measurable reliability. The visual language bridges traditional marketplace warmth with modern institutional confidence.

### Brand Narrative & Emotional Posture
- **Grounded Trust & Longevity:** Deep botanical and earthly references project permanence, stability, and governmental alignment.
- **Empathetic Simplicity ("딸깍" Experience):** Interactions eliminate cognitive strain. Workflows mimic straightforward physical tools where every action is definitive, forgiving, and immediate.
- **Warmth Without Clutter:** Rich cream surfaces and tactile separation replace cold corporate gray planes, making technology feel like a local community bulletin rather than an intimidating software terminal.

### Design Archetype: Humanist Civic Modernism
The design system combines high-contrast functionalism with soft, organic tactile details:
- **Card-centric Modular Hierarchy:** Information is packaged into clear, well-spaced white cards over tinted cream canvas backgrounds.
- **Generous Physical Affordance:** Buttons, toggle rows, and input surfaces favor pronounced vertical heights, distinct pill tags, and tactile active states.
- **Unambiguous Microcopy Anchors:** Visual density remains restrained, offering ample negative space around critical numbers, verification badges, and primary calls to action.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in local sustainability while ensuring high legibility under varying mobile screen conditions and direct sunlight.

### Core Semantic Roles
- **Primary (`#1B4332` / `#2D6A4F`):** Deep Forest Green conveys institution-grade assurance, stability, and growth. Used for key headlines, dominant brand containers, verified government partner marks, and primary headers.
- **Secondary (`#8B5A2B` / `#A0522D`):** Warm Earth Terracotta reflects clay tiles, wooden store signboards, and human heritage. Reserved for supportive badges, thematic categorization, and localized editorial markers.
- **Tertiary & Conversion CTA (`#10B981` with selective `#F59E0B` Amber accents):** Vibrant vitality accents. Sage Lime and Vivid Amber are applied specifically to floating actions, completion states, and critical '딸깍' triggers requiring immediate tap recognition.
- **Neutral & Canvas Surfaces:**
  - `Canvas Base`: `#FDFBF7` (Warm Cream White) reduces screen glare and eye fatigue.
  - `Card Surface`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Pure White) produces crisp contrast separation.
  - `Surface Muted`: `#F4EFEA` (Subtle Oat Neutral) denotes inactive fields and layered list groupings.
  - `Borders & Separators`: `#E8E2D9` delivers gentle structure without harsh black gridlines.

### Text Contrast Hierarchy
- `Text Primary` (`#191C1E`): High-density charcoal for titles and key data metrics, achieving AAA contrast against cream surfaces.
- `Text Body` (`#33383F`): Deep neutral gray calibrated to prevent visual vibration while maintaining high legibility.
- `Text Secondary & Helper` (`#666F75`): Accessible slate for supplementary operational metadata, dates, and form labels.

## Typography

The type scale prioritizes legibility for senior store owners while retaining structural refinement for formal project evaluation.

### Typeface Selection & Font Pairing
- **Headlines (`Public Sans`):** Provides a clean, institutional, and sturdy grotesque structure. It projects public integrity, order, and unambiguous numeric delivery across dashboards and section titles.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Offers a warm, open-aperture geometry that optimizes small-to-medium screen readability, matching the generous proportions and clarity expected of contemporary Korean system UI (such as Pretendard fallback stacks).

### Key Execution Rules
- **Base Body Floor:** Running body copy must not drop below `17px` (`body-lg`) in merchant-facing flows.
- **Generous Vertical Breathing:** Line heights are maintained between `155%` and `165%` for running text to prevent line skipping or visual fatigue.
- **Tabular Numerics:** All transaction metrics, subsidies, and visit counters must activate tabular figure variants (`font-feature-settings: "tnum"`) to maintain tidy vertical alignment across data cards.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid model anchors key content on handheld devices while stepping up gracefully to structured, multi-column desktop representations for administrative and public agency audits.

### Viewport Architecture
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout. Margin set to `1.25rem` (20px) with `1rem` column gutters. Components span full canvas width minus safe padding. Fixed floating navigation and interactive footers remain docked above standard operating system gesture bars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Grid gutters expand to `1.25rem`. Content cards group logically into dual-column cards to maintain comfortable horizontal line length.
- **Desktop (>= 1025px):** 12-column layout maxed at `1120px` container width. Left-hand institutional context/metrics panel paired with interactive demonstration surfaces.

### Spatial Rhythms
- **Element Clearance:** Interactive inputs and cards are spaced strictly using `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) gaps to eliminate accidental mis-taps.
- **Bottom Clearance Buffer:** Mobile screens enforce a mandatory `6rem` (96px) bottom margin pad to account for persistent sticky CTA blocks and prevent content obscuration.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth relies on warm tonal layering and soft, diffused perimeter shadows rather than hard structural planes. This approach mimics physical paper sheets resting lightly on an organized wooden desk.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas (Level 0):** `#FDFBF7`. Flat textured backdrop establishing warm environmental tone.
- **Resting Container (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-subtle border (`1px solid #E8E2D9`) and an ambient drop shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Raised & Interactive Cards (Level 2):** Applied during card selection or modal presentation:
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08), 0 3px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Actions (Level 3):** Reserved for the mobile bottom action bar and verification drawers:
  - `box-shadow: 0 -4px 20px 0 rgba(25, 28, 30, 0.08)`. Features an ambient backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) when partial transparency is applied.

### Tone Isolation
Never use harsh pure black (`#000000`) for elevation shadows; shadow tinting is always derived from the deep forest green base to maintain chromatic unity.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a friendly, approachable geometric curvature that feels natural to the hand and non-intimidating to non-technical users.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Standard UI Controls (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Checkboxes, form fields, small sub-cards, and data pills.
- **Large Content Cards & Modules (`1rem` / 16px):** Primary business insight cards, public record modules, and testimonial panels.
- **Hero Banners & Mobile Sheets (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Modal sheets, persistent bottom action sheets, and highlight callout blocks.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Status badges, tag filters, verification seals, and primary conversion buttons. Full pill rounding reinforces physical push-button feedback.

## Components

Components are engineered with an emphasis on oversized touch regions, clear affordance, and instant cognitive confirmation.

### Buttons
- **Touch Target Floor:** Primary and secondary actions must have a minimum vertical touch height of `52px` (desktop: `48px`).
- **Primary / '딸깍' Action Button:**
  - Background: `#10B981` or deep `#1B4332` depending on visual prominence hierarchy.
  - Text: High-contrast white `#FFFFFF` with bold `17px` weight.
  - Padding: `0 24px`. Full pill-shaped radius (`9999px`).
  - Active State: Physical scale transformation down to `0.98` with subtle inset darkening.
- **Secondary Action Button:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1.5px solid #E8E2D9`. Text: `#1B4332`.
- **Utility / Tertiary Button:**
  - Background: `#F4EFEA`. Text: `#33383F`. Radius: `8px`.

### Floating Bottom CTA Bar (Mobile First)
- **Structure:** Persistent full-width bar anchored to the mobile viewport base.
- **Dimensions:** Height of `76px` plus device-safe bottom padding.
- **Styling:** `#FFFFFF` surface tint with upper border (`1px solid #E8E2D9`) and Level 3 upward ambient shadow. Contains a left-aligned dual line ("사장님 전용 평생 무료 혜택" + "지금 시작하세요") alongside an oversized pill button labeled "3초 만에 딸깍 시작하기".

### Cards & Modular Containers
- **Background:** Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) sitting over the `#FDFBF7` canvas.
- **Border:** `1px solid #E8E2D9`.
- **Padding:** Mobile uses `1.25rem` (20px); desktop uses `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Sub-grouping:** Internal sections use `#FDFBF7` backings with rounded-lg (`12px`) inner corners for structured readability.

### Trust Badges & Verification Ribbons
- **Public Proof Strip:** Fixed-height ribbon highlighting partnerships (e.g., "동작구/마포구 공식 파트너십", "소상공인시장진흥공단 연계").
- **Badge Shape:** Compact pill (`padding: 4px 12px; border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Colors:** Deep Forest Green tint background (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`) with `#1B4332` text, paired with a small institutional checkmark icon.

### Form Inputs & Entry Fields
- **Vertical Height:** `52px` to accommodate thumb taps without accuracy strain.
- **Default State:** White background with `1.5px solid #E8E2D9`. Text: `#191C1E` at `17px`.
- **Focus State:** `1.5px solid #1B4332` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12)`).
- **Labels:** Positioned outside and above the field in bold `15px` (`#191C1E`) with persistent helper cues in `#666F75`.

### Checkboxes & Segment Selectors
- **Tap Boundary:** Outer invisible bounding box of at least `48px x 48px`. Visual checkbox box is oversized to `24px x 24px` with `6px` radius.
- **State Feedback:** Selected states use `#1B4332` fill with a prominent `3px` white stroke checkmark for quick verification by older eyes.
